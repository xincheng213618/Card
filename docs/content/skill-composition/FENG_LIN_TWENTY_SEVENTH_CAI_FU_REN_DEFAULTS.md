## Equipment donation

- All equipment in the source's equipment area must be paid as one frozen actual batch. An empty equipment area, or an area containing any generated `IsGeneralWeapon` entity whose native removal destroys it, suppresses the entire activation. Ordinary equipment is never donated as a subset while a generated weapon remains.
- The owner selects one other living recipient before payment. The game-wide limited usage is consumed after the actual Equipment → Processing → recipient Hand ledger is frozen. X is the count of distinct equipment entities actually delivered to that recipient. A child pause cannot pay again. Paid movement and usage remain paid if either skill source or recipient becomes invalid later.
- Movement children, including Silver Lion recovery/replacement, finish before the recipient chooses. The recipient may recover the skill owner by X or choose zero through X distinct other living characters currently in the recipient's attack range. The range is checked when selecting and confirming the targets, then frozen. Damage source is the recipient, not the skill owner. Targets are resolved in the chosen order; death skips an unpaid target and a winner cancels the unpaid tail.
- Source loss before payment cancels without movement. Source loss after payment leaves the paid entities and usage intact and cancels remaining benefits. Recovery and damage use the existing native HP/damage engines and typed returns; there is no direct HP write in this new capability.
- The limited opportunity belongs to the owner's skill ID for this game. Losing and reacquiring a source instance does not restore an already paid opportunity. Moving an equipped Wooden Ox through Processing follows the existing grain-discard rule; those derived grain movements are not additional donated equipment and do not increase X.
- Native damage modification and exact damage redirection remain active. The issued target and cursor stay frozen; a changed current damage recipient requires the native transfer fact from that original target with the same damage owner and recipient source. This normal-damage operation does not manufacture elemental chain damage, Slash use, card quotas or target-use history.

## Other actual turn ended

- Qualification uses the original completed actual turn number and owner, never the current phase's play counter. Applied damage counts only positive, non-source-less DamageApplied facts from that owner to a different character.
- Actual use targets are recorded from real CardUseDeclared/TargetsConfirmed and their exact owning CardUse frame. Actor and provider remain distinct. Ordinary Slash/Dodge responses do not count. Nullification targets a card rather than a character, so its opponent/requester fields are not invented as character targets. Actual action-null virtual Slash/Alcohol producers retain their native action-null identity and count only their confirmed actual character targets.
- The second clause is an independent tier: no use on another character allows two different options, even if a separate skill caused damage. Otherwise no damage to another character allows one; both disqualifying facts allow none. Each option is used at most once. If there is no legally movable current equipment, only the draw option is available. A two-option tier resolves all available distinct options, in the owner's chosen order; it cannot draw twice.
- Equipment is placed directly in the owner's current corresponding slot with the existing replacement, equipment grant, and Silver Lion removal behavior. Native generated weapons that cannot survive transfer are ineligible. This is placement, not a new equipment card use. No judgment or hand entities are exposed or selectable.
- Wooden Ox grain transfer follows the existing direct Equipment-to-Equipment rule, including the native removal of a replaced owner's Ox grain. Derived grain movements are checked against that exact paid equipment batch and are not independent option payments.
- The foreign chosen equipment must survive transfer. A generated weapon already in the owner's replaced slot follows native destruction to OutsideGame, and only that exact frozen replaced ID and original generated flag are accepted with EquipmentReplace. An ordinary replaced entity must move to DiscardPile. If this real replacement removes the skill's exact source instance, the paid placement remains and the unpaid second option is cancelled.
- The qualification is frozen at the actual TurnEnded boundary; subsequent benefit children do not retroactively change that original turn's tier. Source loss/death or game over cancels unpaid options; paid draw/placement does not roll back. The original source instance/hash and exact after-turn parent/candidate remain required after JSON restoration. Facts collection reference equality is never used for this new opt-in path.
