## Integration limits

- Final OLD remains a separate gate: preserve legacy 6303 behavior, old enum values, nullable/omitted receipt shape, exact 6800 recipe eligibility/receipt/selection unions, dead-owner op admission, pre-Death-return assertion placement and completion hook immediately before pop.
- A second simultaneous living victim's Dying is explicitly unsupported by the author's contract. This review does not propose hiding any living Dying or expanding the scheduler.
- Layered dead-original returns and Alcohol families were examined statically; the two added scenarios do not execute a second OwnerDied-benefit layer or every rescue family. They must not be described as runtime-covered.
- No author or main file was changed. No compiler, build, production loader, tests, native simulation, benchmark, HTTP, media download, full catalog scan or full input-hash scan was executed.
