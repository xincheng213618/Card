# Engineering defaults, not official FAQ

- The current primary text is authoritative. The first20 cache is discovery/identity context only. No historical skill body is substituted.
- The accepted final default supersedes the initial non-frozen draft: after the real deposit, skill loss alone does not revoke the original obligation while its source remains alive. The original source/hash/deposit/candidate proves it, without granting a replacement skill instance. Source or target death removes the real still-held card without charging target HP; a dead target has no invented chooser. After exchange/removal is paid, issued children drain once before death/winner cancels unissued successors.
- Independently issued offers resolve in original deposit-ID order. New offers created during that same actual start wait for a later turn. Extra and face-down skipped actual turns both qualify as next turn starts.
- Matching type is printed Basic/Trick/Equipment, not transformed effective use identity. Payment is Hand only. Removal sends the held card to DiscardPile. New public events never contain a still-private material ID.
- Songci discards legal HE, insufficient means all legal HE. Generated equipment destruction follows mature exact OutsideGame movement and counts as one legal discarded entity. Its branch freezes at issuance; equality is unavailable.
- Songci quota is owner+skill+state+target for the entire game, independent of source instance. It is consumed at issuance, even if terminal state later cancels an unissued reward. A unique GameTargetHandHpChoiceIssuedEvent in the mature complete rule-fact history preserves the issuance after ResetSkill clears its ordinary game-usage entry. Eligibility always checks that durable issued key first; no broad ResetSkill is used by this mechanism.
- Physical PrivateTurnHold ownership remains source-owned, but typed deferred offers are projected on the logical target's PrivateTurnHolds, with DeferredSourceSeat. The source retains original-card knowledge; the target sees it only in its exact due private prompt. Trusted revealAll remains the existing diagnostic override. Old ordinary private holds keep owner projection and ordinary expiry.
- No FAQ was obtained for source loss, multiple offers, skipped starts, discard region/insufficiency or terminal cancellation. These remain engineering choices for later verification.
- Compiler, build, production loader, gameplay, tests, native AI, UI and benchmark execution are false. No publish, release, push, commit or version update is included.
