# Engineering defaults; current official FAQ not established

The complete current page text is authoritative; the following decisions fill unspecified execution details and must not be presented as official FAQ.

- The optional opportunity occurs once at this exact actual owner Ending item. Skipped Ending offers nothing. An extra actual turn has its own Ending; inserting a phase alone does not invent another actual turn. No inherited once-per-game limiter is added.
- “装备牌” uses the real printed equipment kind/slot, not a temporary effective Slash/conversion identity. Source zones are actual Hand or Equipment. Placement is a move into Equipment, not Use. The recipient may be any legal living character including owner if source is Hand; moving an equipped entity back into the same equipment area is excluded by existing placement legality.
- A generated general weapon is excluded as a placement source because the native Equipment departure destroys it to OutsideGame, so it cannot be delivered. This is a narrow actual-entity rule, not a historical same-name substitution. An ordinary equipment may replace a generated weapon; that exact replaced entity goes OutsideGame. A weapon benefit may discard generated equipped weapon through the same destruction boundary; it is never falsely logged into DiscardPile.
- Region means Hand/Equipment/Judgment (HEJ). Owner is the chooser. A foreign Hand is represented by private opaque slot; only accepted actual discard reveals its entity. Shared discard prevention remains effective. Equipment placement Transfer does not become self-discard and is not blocked by a Hand-category discard ban.
- Weapon qualification uses recipient→target current final directed distance after every initial placement/replacement child finishes, and revalidates before discard. Dead seats and all existing/new distance policies are read through GetCombatDistance; no raw-seat or reverse-direction shortcut is used. A recipient has distance0 to itself. The owner may itself be a qualifying distance1 target. If no target/eligible card remains, the paid placement completes without a fabricated discard.
- Unpaid choice may cancel, and no movement/reward fact is issued. Before payment, missing source/illegal entity/recipient cancels without payment. After actual placement, simple skill loss/suppression or reacquisition cannot revoke the promised slot benefit or charge again; the original instance/hash/item owns it.
- After placement, owner death prevents further interactive weapon choice and finishes the already paid placement. Armor/mount benefits can still complete for a living recipient after owner death if the game continues. Recipient death or a winner before reward issuance cancels the unissued benefit; already issued movement/recovery/observer tails are drained. No reward is fabricated to justify cancellation.
- The slot frozen at real placement determines the branch even if later children move that equipment elsewhere. Armor Draw1 may actually draw0 from an exhausted deck and records its exact attempted invoice. It does not manufacture a card or a Drawn fact when no attempt was made. Mount uses the eligible recovery amount min(1,lostHP); at full HP it makes no HP gain event. Replacement policies remain native and cannot re-pay equipment.
- Treasure equipment can be placed through existing slot legality; the current text names only weapon/armor/mount benefits, so Treasure has no additional benefit. This is an explicit inference where no current FAQ was established.
- AI decisions use owner-visible material and public relationship/HP/distance/equipment/card-count information. Foreign hidden IDs/kinds are not consulted; opaque hand slots use deterministic public ordering. The optional trigger uses a conservative GainCards prior and recipient/weapon choices use the existing public relationship scorer. This is an engineering heuristic, not a measured win rate.
- Cold means JSON accepted-journal checkpoint restoration and actual continued commands on the restored instance. No claim is made that checkpoint fields serialize a live frame graph or that a replay alias bug was reproduced.
